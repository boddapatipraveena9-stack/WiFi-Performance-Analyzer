1. About the Project

WiFi performance depends on signal strength, interference, channel width, congestion and the 802.11 standard in use. This project explains these parameters and gives simple tools to analyze them.

Objectives: study WLAN performance metrics, measure latency and jitter, estimate signal loss from distance and obstacles, and recommend ways to improve a network.

5. Channel Overlap (2.4 GHz)

Only channels 1, 6 and 11 do not overlap. Pick a channel and see which neighbours it interferes with (channels are 5 MHz apart, each about 22 MHz wide).

Real-world throughput is usually 40–60 % of the theoretical rate because of protocol overhead (CSMA/CA, ACKs) and shared airtime.

7. How to Improve WiFi Performance

  • Place the router centrally and high, away from walls and metal.
  • Use 5 GHz for speed and short range, 2.4 GHz for coverage.
  • Choose a non-overlapping channel (1, 6 or 11 on 2.4 GHz).
  • Keep firmware updated and use WPA3/WPA2 security.
  • Reduce interference from microwaves, Bluetooth and cordless phones.
  • Add mesh nodes or access points for large areas.
